D — V-leg boltMARK D · LOCKED
Voltra
1 & 2 are two-tone: left ink / live lime 3 is mono accent — one continuous stroke any variant can render all-ink or all-lime

“V-leg bolt” — one straight leg (the reliable line) and one live leg (the current). V and bolt resolve into a single glyph: reads V at a glance, bolt at a second glance. Two-tone version tells the brand story on its own. Asymmetric, so it shines large — in the header and hero rather than at favicon size.

Winner: 1 · straight — butt caps, miter join, one notch, two-tone (ink left leg / lime live leg) — locked in PLAN.md §8 and shown in the lockup above. Executions 2 (round) and 3 (squared) are kept as alternates here for future reference.

E — Twin-strike VMARK E · NEW
Voltra
both legs accent both legs ink-hi legs ink-hi + accent apex bolt tail

“Twin-strike V” — the bolt’s notch on both legs, perfectly symmetric. Reads V instantly, and the doubled zigzag reads voltage / energy / two-phase power. The most geometric and ownable silhouette in the family; the notches stay readable at small sizes better than D, though it’s still a glyph mark, not a tile.
